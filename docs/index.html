Free firmware for the EP–2350 Ting and FX Mic

Autotune your voice. Record your own samples. All on the mic.

fx-mic-mod gives Teenage Engineering's little effects mic two new tricks. Autotune now stacks with distortion, reverb, echo and the rest. Record mode captures new samples straight into the mic, with no computer involved.

Autotune that stacks Snap to every note or to a key you choose, from a hard robot snap to a gentle nudge. Put it on the handle. Record samples on the mic Four slots, four seconds each. Squeeze to record, tap to play it back. No cable, no app.
Install the firmware Design presets in your browser

Early release. The firmware is tested on a computer with simulated hardware but hasn't run on a real mic yet. Back up your mic first. The install guide does that for you, and you can always go back to the stock firmware.

Feature 1 · Autotune

Pitch-perfect, then as weird as you like.

Autotune listens to your voice and pulls it onto the right note. It always runs first, so whatever comes after (distortion, a pitch-shifted harmony, a long echo, a big reverb) gets a perfectly tuned voice to work with.

Key

Every note, or your key

Chromatic snaps to the nearest semitone. Pick major or minor in any of the 12 keys, and you only ever land on notes that belong to the song.

Strength

Hard snap to natural

One slider goes from the instant, robotic jump to a gentle glide that keeps your vibrato. Humanize leaves small wobbles alone.

Handle

Squeeze to tune harder

Map the strength to the handle in the editor. Sing lightly for a natural voice, then clamp down for the full robot.

New · Handle

Squeeze to change key

Give a preset a second key. Sing in C major, squeeze all the way and you're in C minor, ease off and you're back. Or go from every-note to a locked key. Made for playing live.

Feature 2 · Record mode

Sample anything, right on the mic.

The stock mic only plays samples you copy over from a computer. With fx-mic-mod you can fill its four sample slots out in the world: a friend's laugh, a beatbox loop, a door slam. The mic's own lights walk you through it.

● REC 0.0 s
4 slots × 4 sEach take replaces the sample in its slot, the same 1.wav to 4.wav you'd copy over by hand.
Effects includedGive each slot its own effects in the editor, and your new recording plays through them.
Kept after power-offTakes are saved to the mic's drive, ready to copy to your computer later.

How it fits together

STEP 1 · ONCE

Install the firmware

About 15 minutes, on a computer with Chrome or Edge.

  • Back up the samples and settings on your mic
  • Put CircuitPython on it
  • Copy the fx-mic-mod files over in one click
Install guide
STEP 2 · ANY TIME

Design your presets

The editor only changes your presets. The firmware stays as it is.

  • Build up to four effect chains, with autotune first if you want it
  • Hear them live through your computer's mic
  • Save config.json to the mic
Preset editor
STEP 3

Sing, play and record

Unplug it, or leave it on USB power.

  • Step through your presets with the orange button
  • Squeeze the handle and sing
  • Record your own samples into the four slots
Button guide

On the mic

Orange: next presetClean → preset 1 to 4 → record mode → clean. The red lights show which preset is on.
White: next sample slotSlots 1 to 4, shown on the white lights.
Grey: play the sampleTap to play, hold to play, or tap start/stop, set per slot in the editor.
Handle: squeeze to singOpens the mic. Presets can also use how far you squeeze: more autotune, a key change, a deeper pitch, more echo.
+
Orange and white together, about 1 secondTurns the mic off.

Saving files to the mic

Normal power-on: the mic can save

Recording works, and your computer sees the mic's drive as read-only.

Hold orange while you power on: your computer can save

Use this edit mode to save presets from the editor or copy WAV files over. The red lights flash in record mode to show recording is off until you power on normally again.

A take replaces that slot's sample

Copy a sample to your computer first if you want to keep it.
