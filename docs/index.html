EP–2350 Ting · CircuitPython · open source

Stack autotune with any effect on your Ting.

fx-mic-mod replaces the Ting's firmware. You get four presets that chain autotune, distortion, reverb, delay, chorus and more, an editor to design them in your browser, and a record mode that captures samples on the mic itself.

Install the firmware Open the preset editor

Early release. The firmware is tested on a computer with simulated hardware but hasn't run on a real Ting yet. Back up your mic first. The install guide does that for you, and you can always go back to the stock firmware.

How it fits together

STEP 1 · ONCE

Install the firmware

About 15 minutes, on a computer with Chrome or Edge.

  • Back up the samples and settings on your Ting
  • Put CircuitPython on it
  • Copy the fx-mic-mod files over in one click
Install guide
STEP 2 · ANY TIME

Design your presets

The editor only changes your presets. The firmware stays as it is.

  • Build up to four effect chains, with autotune first if you want it
  • Hear them live through your computer's mic
  • Save config.json to the Ting
Preset editor
STEP 3

Play and record

Unplug it, or leave it on USB power.

  • Step through your presets with the orange button
  • Squeeze the handle and sing
  • Record your own samples into the four slots
Button guide

On the mic

Orange: next presetClean → preset 1 to 4 → record mode → clean. The red LEDs show which preset is on.
White: next sample slotSlots 1 to 4, shown on the white LEDs.
Grey: play the sampleTap to play, hold to play, or tap start/stop, set per slot in the editor.
Handle: squeeze to singOpens the mic. Presets can also use how far you squeeze, for example from natural to hard autotune.
+
Orange and white together, about 1 secondTurns the Ting off.

Record mode

Get there

Press orange past preset 4. All four red LEDs light and the slot's white LED blinks.

Record

Pick a slot with white, then hold the handle and talk. The red LEDs count down the 4 seconds . Let go to save, then press grey to hear it.

A take replaces that slot's sample

Copy the old sample to your computer first if you want to keep it.

Saving files to the Ting

Normal power-on: the mic can save

Recording works, and your computer sees the Ting's drive as read-only.

Hold orange while you power on: your computer can save

Use this edit mode to save presets from the editor or copy WAV files over. Recording is off until you power on normally again.
