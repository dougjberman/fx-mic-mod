Late-thirties engineering · firmware mod for EP–2350 Ting / FX Mic

Autotune your voice. Record your own samples. All on the mic.

fx-mic-mod gives Teenage Engineering's little fx mic three big new tricks. Autotune now stacks with distortion, reverb, echo and the rest. A harmonizer adds backing singers in any chord. Record mode captures new samples straight into the mic, with no computer involved.

Autotune that stacks Snap to every note or lock to a key, from a hard robot snap to a gentle nudge. You can even map the snap strength or key changes to the handle. Backing singers on tap Pick a chord, from plain major to maj7, 9ths and 13ths, and up to three voices sing it with you on every note. Record samples on the mic F it. We'll do it live. Squeeze to record, tap to play it back. No cable, no computer necessary.

Feature 1 · Autotune

Pitch-perfect, then as weird as you like.

Autotune listens to your voice and pulls it onto the right note. It always runs first, so every effect after it gets a voice that's already in tune. Then pile on distortion, a harmony, a long echo or a huge reverb.

What autotune does to a sung line
What you sangWhat comes out, in C major
Key

Every note, or your key

Chromatic snaps to the nearest semitone. Pick major or minor in any of the 12 keys, and you only land on notes in that key.

Strength

Hard snap to natural

One slider goes from the instant, robotic jump to a gentle glide that keeps your vibrato. Humanize leaves small wobbles alone.

Handle

Make the squeeze do more

Squeezing the handle always turns the mic on. In the editor you can give it one more job in each autotune preset, either of these:

Tune harder

A light squeeze sounds natural. Clamp down for full robot.

Change key

Sing in C major, squeeze hard for C minor, ease off to go back. Or go from every note to a locked key.

Feature 2 · Harmonizer

Bring your own backing singers.

Pick a chord and up to three more voices sing it with you, built on every note you sing. They follow autotune, so the harmony lands in tune, and the squeeze can bring them in only when you want them.

One sung line, four voices
YouBacking voices
Chords

38 chords, any voicing

Major and minor, sus2 and sus4, 6ths, every kind of 7th, add9, 9ths, 11ths, 13ths and the Hendrix 7♯9. The editor shows the exact notes before you pick.

In the key

Harmony that knows the key

Set autotune to a key and the chord follows the scale, like a real vocal harmonizer: sing E in C major and you get E minor, sing F and you get F major.

Voicing

Above you or under you

Inversions tuck chord notes below your voice so you sing the top line. An octave switch moves the whole choir up or down.

Squeeze

Squeeze for backups

Sing alone with a light squeeze, clamp down and the choir comes in. Preset 2 in the editor is set up this way.

Build a harmony in the editor
Feature 3 · Record mode

Sample anything, right on the mic.

The stock mic only plays samples you copy over from a computer. With fx-mic-mod you can fill its four sample slots out in the world: a friend's laugh, a beatbox loop, a door slam. The mic's own lights walk you through it.

● REC 0.0 s
4 slots × 4 sEach take replaces the sample in its slot, the same 1.wav to 4.wav you'd copy over by hand.
Through your presetsSamples play through whichever effect preset is selected on the mic, autotune and all. Or give each slot its own effects in the editor.
Kept after power-offTakes are saved to the mic's drive, ready to copy to your computer later.

How it fits together

STEP 1 · ONCE

Install the firmware

About 15 minutes, on a computer with Chrome or Edge.

  • Back up the samples and settings on your mic
  • Put CircuitPython on it
  • Copy the fx-mic-mod files over in one click
Install guide
STEP 2 · ANY TIME

Design your presets

With the fx-mic-mod installed, set up your new toy.

  • Build your autotune and effects chains using the web UI
  • Try them live using your computer's mic
  • Save the config.json file to the mic
Preset Editor
STEP 3

Sing, play and record

Play time begins.

  • Step through your presets with the orange button
  • Squeeze the handle and sing
  • Record your own samples into the four slots
Button guide

On the mic

Orange: next presetClean → preset 1 to 4 → record mode → clean. The red lights count up like the stock mic: one for preset 1, all four for preset 4, all four flashing in record mode.
White: next sample slotSlots 1 to 4, counted on the white lights the same way.
Grey: play the sampleTap to play, hold to play, or tap start/stop, set per slot in the editor.
Handle: squeeze to singOpens the mic. Presets can also use how far you squeeze: more autotune, a key change, a deeper pitch, more echo.
Orange and white together, about 1 secondTurns the mic off.

Saving files to the mic

Mic recording enabled (Normal mode)

Power on normally: squeeze the handle

  • Samples can be recorded through the mic
  • If connected, your computer sees the mic drive as read-only
  • Samples can be copied from the mic drive to the computer if you want to keep them
Computer-edit mode

Hold orange while you squeeze the handle to power on

  • Use this edit mode to load presets from the editor or copy WAV files over
  • Note: in record mode the red lights flash in alternating pairs, instead of all together, to show recording is off until you power the mic on normally again (just squeeze the handle)
