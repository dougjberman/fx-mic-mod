Design your presets

Build the four effect chains on the orange button, hear them through your computer's mic, then save config.json to the mic.

How to use this page

  1. 1 · DESIGNPick a preset and build its chainEach card below is one press of the orange button. Start from a template or add effects. Autotune always runs first.
  2. 2 · LISTENTry it with your computer's micClick Use my mic and wear wired headphones (Bluetooth adds a delay the real mic doesn't have). Every change you make plays right away. Drag the Squeeze slider to try squeeze settings like a key change.
  3. 3 · SAVEPut it on the micHold orange while you squeeze the handle to power on the mic, plug it in, then click Save to mic and pick CIRCUITPY. Power it off, then squeeze the handle on its own to power on and play.

This page only changes your presets (config.json). If your mic doesn't have fx-mic-mod yet, follow the install guide first.

Presets · orange button

Pack name

The orange button steps through Clean, presets 1 to 4, then Record mode. Squeeze the handle to hear the mic.

Volume between presets. Distortion and reverb can make one preset much louder than another. Sing the same phrase through each preset and watch the meter in Try it, then set each preset's Output level so the peak line lands in about the same place. The preview only estimates the mic, so check by ear on the mic too: switch between Clean and each preset while you sing, and turn down any that jump out.

Samples · white and grey buttons

White picks a slot, grey plays it. To record a slot on the mic, step the orange button past preset 4 into Record mode, pick the slot with white, and hold the handle while you talk (up to 4 seconds).
