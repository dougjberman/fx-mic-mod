How to use this page

One file holds everything on this page. Your four effect presets and your sample settings all live in config.json, and Save to mic replaces the whole file. To change one part and keep the rest, start from the mic's own file (step 1). Your recordings (1.wav to 4.wav) are separate files: saving leaves them as they are. To swap one, record over it on the mic or use the sample copier.

  1. 1 · STARTLoad what's on the micTurn the mic on in edit mode (hold orange while you squeeze the handle) and plug it in. Click Open a config.json and pick config.json on CIRCUITPY. The page now matches the mic, so whatever you don't change stays as it was. Skip this only if you want to start fresh.
  2. 2 · EFFECTSBuild and hear your presetsBuild four unique effects chain presets, one per press of the orange button. Start from a template or add effects; autotune always runs first. Click Use my mic and wear wired headphones (Bluetooth adds a delay the real mic doesn't have). Drag the Squeeze slider to try squeeze settings.
  3. 3 · SAMPLESSet up the white button's slotsClick Copy samples from the mic to hear 1.wav to 4.wav here and through your presets. Pick how each slot plays when you press the button, and set the record-mode options: whether to trim silence and/or normalize recording levels.
  4. 4 · SAVEPut it on the micClick Save to mic and pick CIRCUITPY. This replaces the mic's presets and sample settings with this page. If the mic has settings this page hasn't loaded, it asks first. The mic restarts by itself; to record samples, turn it off, then on again with just a squeeze.

If your mic doesn't have fx-mic-mod yet, follow the install guide first. New sample WAVs go on with the sample copier or by recording on the mic.

Presets · orange button

Pack name

The orange button steps through Clean, presets 1 to 4, then Record mode. Squeeze the handle to hear the mic.

Volume between presets. Distortion and reverb can make one preset much louder than another. Sing the same phrase through each preset and watch the meter in Try it, then set each preset's Output level so the peak line lands in about the same place. The preview only estimates the mic, so check by ear on the mic too: switch between Clean and each preset while you sing, and turn down any that jump out.

Samples

The four sounds the white button picks and the gray button plays.

Sample slots · white button

There are two ways to get samples onto the mic: copy WAV files from your computer with the sample copier on the Install page, or record them on the mic itself, with no computer needed. On the mic, the white button picks a slot and the gray button plays it.

The WAVs below are only for hearing each slot here, with its effects. Choosing one doesn't change the mic, and Save to mic saves your settings, not the WAVs.

To record a sample directly on the mic:

  • Press the orange button to step past preset 4 into Record mode, where all four red lights start blinking.
  • Use the white button to pick the slot, and hold the handle while you talk (up to 4 seconds).
  • The audio you record takes a second to process and save to the mic, and a light runs down the mic from top to bottom while it does. Silence before and after your audio is trimmed, unless you turn that off below. Takes keep the volume you recorded them at, unless you choose Normalize below.

The gray button

Do thisThe sample
TapPlays all the way through, once. Tap again while it plays to start it over.
Press and holdPlays while you hold, and stops when you let go.
Double-tapPlays backwards from the end. Hold the second tap to stop it when you let go.

Every press starts the sample straight away. A press counts as a hold once it lasts a quarter of a second, and a second tap within about a third of a second makes a double-tap. Use the gray button on each slot below to try it here.
